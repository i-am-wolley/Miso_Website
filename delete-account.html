Account deletion

Delete your Miso account

This page applies to the Miso app (package: app.pantryos), published by I_am_Wolley.

How to delete your account

The fastest way — do this from inside the app.

  1. 1

    Open the Miso app and sign in

  2. 2

    Go to Settings

  3. 3

    Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account

  4. 4

    Confirm — deletion happens immediately, no waiting period

Don't have the app installed?

If you've uninstalled Miso or would rather not open the app, you can still request deletion.

  • Email misoadmin2210@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" from the email address associated with your Miso account

  • Or use the Contact form, topic "Account, billing & subscriptions", stating you want your account deleted

  • Requests are processed within 5 business days

What gets deleted

  • Your account sign-in record and personal profile are deleted immediately

  • You are removed from your household's member list

  • If you are the sole or creating member of your household — or you choose "also delete household data" when deleting in-app — all household data is deleted too: pantry items, saved dishes, weekly menus, recipes, and activity history

  • If you share a household with others and don't choose full deletion, that shared household data (pantry, dishes, menu, recipes) is not deleted, since it belongs to the household rather than just you — it stays in place for your remaining household members

  • No data is retained beyond what's described above — there is no backup or grace-period copy kept after deletion
